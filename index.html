      Questions

      Straight answers.

      What is Robinhood Chain?
      An EVM chain built by Robinhood, chain ID 4663, with gas paid in ETH. Add it to any wallet with RPC https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com, or just press Connect above and this site will add it for you.
      How do I buy $ROBIN?
      Bridge a little ETH to Robinhood Chain, connect your wallet, and use the swap panel. You can also trade on Pons or any Uniswap front-end on chain 4663. Only ever use the contract address shown in the header.
      Is the liquidity locked?
      Yes. $ROBIN graduated off the Pons bonding curve into a Uniswap V4 pool whose position is permanently locked and governed by the Pons shared hook. The LP cannot be withdrawn by the team or anyone else.
      Did Billy Markus really get 30M tokens?
      He did — sent from us, unprompted, with nothing asked in return. It's a plain ERC-20 transfer, so you don't have to take our word for it: check the explorer.
      Can the supply change?
      No. The Pons factory mints a fixed one billion at deploy and there is no mint function afterwards. What exists today is all there will ever be.
      What does the AI actually do?
      It reads the same live market data this page shows and answers questions about the token, the chain and how to trade it. Your OpenRouter key stays on the server — the browser only ever talks to your own api/ai endpoint.
      Is this financial advice?
      Absolutely not. $ROBIN is a memecoin with no roadmap promises and no expectation of profit. Prices go to zero all the time. Only ever risk what you'd shrug off losing.
